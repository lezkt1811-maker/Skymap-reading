How this works

Each planet on your chart wheel is matched to a real sky photo taken at the same moment. Work through steps 1–4 in order. The banner above the wheel always shows what to do next.

Your images stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

1 Upload your chart wheel
Wheel image
2 Enter the planet list

One planet per line, like Sun · 31°59' Sagittarius. Type it, paste it, or upload a screenshot of the list to read it automatically.

3 Add a sky photo for each planet

For each planet: upload its sky screenshot, then press Draw box on wheel and drag a box around that planet's symbol on the wheel.

4 Point the arrows and lock them

Click a planet to open it. Drag the arrow onto the planet on the wheel, then onto the same planet in the sky photo. Press Lock when it is right.

5 Export or share optional
